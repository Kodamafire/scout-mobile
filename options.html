One small loss can erase one small win. That is why Scout needs at least 100 test trades with positive results after costs before these settings are trusted.

How the numbers are worked out
  • Cost = price paid × 100 shares. $0.90 × 100 = $90.
  • Worth now = current bid × 100. The bid is what a buyer will pay right now.
  • Profit / loss = worth now − $90 paid. Fees are not included.
  • Stop-loss: sell if the bid falls 25% below $0.90, to $0.675.
  • Profit protection starts once the bid is 20% above $0.90, at $1.08. From then on Scout sells if the bid drops 15% from its highest point.
  • A sell order is filled at whatever a buyer pays next, which can be a little lower than the trigger.
